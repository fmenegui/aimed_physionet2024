# License and attribution

Effective date for the new license offers: 2026-10-26.

This maintained release uses noncommercial licenses for new AIMED-owned
software contributions, documentation and the covered checkpoint rights.
Commercial rights outside the licenses' expressly permitted purposes are
reserved for separate authorization. These offers preserve valid earlier
permissions and third-party terms, as explained below.

## New software contributions

New AIMED-owned software contributions in this maintained release are offered
under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Keep the license and
the attribution lines in [NOTICE](NOTICE) when sharing covered software.

Noncommercial use, modification and redistribution are permitted under those
terms. Commercial uses outside the license's expressly permitted purposes
require separate authorization from the relevant rights holders. Contact the
maintainers through this repository for commercial licensing.

The license text is unmodified. Its required attribution notices are provided
separately in NOTICE.

## Documentation and trained artifacts

The documentation and AIMED's copyright interests in the checkpoint artifacts
identified in [weights_manifest.json](weights_manifest.json) are offered under
[CC BY-NC 4.0](LICENSES/CC-BY-NC-4.0.txt). When sharing covered material, retain
attribution, provide the license link and indicate changes. Commercial use
outside an applicable existing permission requires separate authorization.

See [MODEL_LICENSE.md](MODEL_LICENSE.md) for the exact checkpoint scope and
prior-grant exceptions. These terms do not relicense source datasets or
third-party pretrained parameters. The weight downloader places the checkpoint
license, scope notice and attribution alongside the downloaded files.

## Local evaluation and product development

Noncommercial research, evaluation and experimentation are permitted within the
respective software and checkpoint grants, subject to their conditions. Local
execution, public or synthetic inputs and a nonclinical setting do not by
themselves establish a noncommercial purpose.

The CC BY-NC checkpoint offer does not authorize commercial inference services,
commercial product integration or feasibility evaluations primarily intended
to develop a commercial product. These uses require separate authorization,
unless an applicable existing permission already covers them, even before a
sale or clinical deployment. CC's
[definition and guidance](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#does-my-use-violate-the-noncommercial-clause-of-the-licenses)
address the intended advantage and circumstances, rather than the user's
organization type alone.

For product-directed evaluation, establish authorization with the relevant
rights holders unless an applicable existing permission covers it. PolyForm's
expressly permitted purposes must be read in its own text. They do not enlarge
the separate CC BY-NC checkpoint grant. Earlier permissions remain in force
as described below. This documentation makes no additional commercial-evaluation
grant.

## Historical 2024 code and prior permissions

This policy does not revoke or narrow rights already granted under BSD, MIT
or any other applicable license.

The [2024 Challenge rules](https://moody-challenge.physionet.org/2024/#intellectual-property-and-open-source-licenses)
say that a submission without a specified license is treated as BSD-3-Clause.
The inspected source snapshot has no root license file and has historical MIT
metadata in its package configuration. The exact terms accompanying the
evaluated submission and earlier checkpoint distributions have not been
independently established here.

Consequently, the historical 2024 implementation is not represented as becoming
commercial-use prohibited. A new license cannot be relied on to withdraw an
earlier permission. Original portions retain their applicable terms, while the
new noncommercial grant covers AIMED-owned contributions and rights that can
be offered on these terms.

The imported file provenance is recorded in [SOURCE.json](SOURCE.json).
The publication and audit commits identify the later changes.

## Third-party material

The Challenge scaffold's BSD-2-Clause notice is retained in
[LICENSES/PhysioNet-BSD-2-Clause.txt](LICENSES/PhysioNet-BSD-2-Clause.txt). It covers
the corresponding upstream material, including the official runners, helpers,
evaluation and preparation code. Its existing permission is not replaced by
the new license.

Libraries, ECG-Image-Kit, datasets and third-party starting weights retain
their own licenses. No private InCor data are distributed.
