## Historical 2024 code and prior permissions

This policy does not revoke or narrow rights already granted under BSD, MIT
or any other applicable license.

The [2024 Challenge rules](https://moody-challenge.physionet.org/2024/#intellectual-property-and-open-source-licenses)
say that a submission without a specified license is treated as BSD-3-Clause.
The inspected source snapshot has no root license file and has historical MIT
metadata in its package configuration. The exact terms accompanying the
evaluated submission and earlier checkpoint distributions have not been
independently established here.

Consequently, the historical 2024 implementation is not represented as becoming
commercial-use prohibited. A new license cannot be relied on to withdraw an
earlier permission. Original portions retain their applicable terms, while the
new noncommercial grant covers AIMED-owned contributions and rights that can
be offered on these terms.

The imported file provenance is recorded in [SOURCE.json](SOURCE.json).
The publication and audit commits identify the later changes.
